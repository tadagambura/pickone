# PickOne

**Can't decide? Spin for it**

> #3 of 33 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Ad hoc, kept for good · Very easy · Revenue potential: Low · Social, Play & Style

## What it is

A deliberately silly, quick app for indecisive people, especially couples and friend groups. Add options and spin a wheel, flip a coin, roll dice or run a quick group vote, with daft animations and sounds. Save wheels for decisions you make often (dinner, films) and exclude recent picks so it doesn't keep landing on the same thing.

## Why it's in the portfolio

It's stupid and fun, and that's the point: it takes days to build, it's naturally shareable (people film spins), and indecisive couples keep it on their phone for good. It's a cheap way to bring in users and point them at the bigger apps.

## Who it's for

Couples, friend groups, families, classrooms, anyone stuck choosing.

**The problem:** Small decisions waste time and cause arguments.

## Version one

- Wheel, coin flip, dice and random picker
- Weighted options
- Saved wheels for repeat decisions
- 'Exclude recently chosen'
- Quick group vote (ranked choice)
- Shareable result

**Example:** A group can't agree on dinner, adds five places, spins, and shares the result in the group chat.

## Animation and tone

**Full drama.** Duolingo-style theatrics: characters, big over-the-top reactions, sound effects, comic tragedy ('NOOOOOOO!!!! NOT CYPHICUS!!!!').

- Drumroll while the wheel spins, slowing with suspense; near-misses get gasps
- 'THE WHEEL HAS SPOKEN' reveal with a spotlight and fanfare
- Spin again and the wheel acts offended ('Oh, so my opinion doesn't matter?')

**Line we don't cross:** Keep it short: the whole spin is a few seconds, and skippable.

## AI

**None.** Not needed. Plain code does everything this app does.

## Watch-outs
