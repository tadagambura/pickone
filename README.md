# PickOne

**Can't decide? Spin for it**

> #8 of 33 in the build order · Very easy · Revenue potential: Low · Social, Play & Style

## What it is

Add your options and spin a wheel, flip a coin, roll dice or run a quick group vote. Save wheels for decisions you make often (dinner, films, chores) and exclude recent picks so you don't keep getting the same answer.

## Why it's in the portfolio

One of the fastest apps to ship, and naturally shareable (people film spins). Low revenue on its own, so its real job is cheap acquisition and cross-promoting the stronger apps.

## Who it's for

Couples, friend groups, families, classrooms, anyone stuck choosing.

**The problem:** Small decisions waste time and cause arguments.

## Version one

- Wheel, coin flip, dice and random picker
- Weighted options
- Saved wheels for repeat decisions
- 'Exclude recently chosen'
- Quick group vote (ranked choice)
- Shareable result

**Example:** A group can't agree on dinner, adds five places, spins, and shares the result in the group chat.

## Later

- Premium wheel themes
- Weekly dinner or chore wheel for recurring use

## How it makes money

| Model | Detail |
|---|---|
| Free | Everything basic, with ads |
| Premium | £1.99 lifetime: themes, unlimited saved wheels, no ads |
| Ads | Banner, plus a rewarded ad to try a theme |

## Why people come back

Built on **The Progression Loop**, the portfolio's shared retention framework
(streaks with forgiveness, visible progress, milestones, recaps, no guilt-trips).

| Stage | In PickOne |
|---|---|
| Cue | Moments of indecision (no scheduled cue) |
| Tiny win | Spin |
| Progress | Saved wheels and history |
| Share | Spin results and recordings |
| Next step | Suggest a recurring wheel (weekly dinner) |
| Cadence | Ad hoc |

## Watch-outs

Weak retention and a crowded category. Ship fast, don't over-invest, use it to cross-promote.
